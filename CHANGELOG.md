## 1.0.0

First public-release package.

Highlights include:

- Expanded Kanto world with new towns, routes, caves, dungeons, and postgame areas.
- Large expanded Regional Dex with later-generation Pokemon.
- Reworked encounters, trades, evolution methods, shops, trainers, Gyms, League content, and rematches.
- Indigo City Gym plus Regular and Master's Kanto Fighting Tournaments.
- Extensive side-quest and postgame quest content.
- Custom signature moves and battle animations.
- Journal / Adventure Record system.
- Postgame legendary arcs for Celebi, Ho-Oh, Lugia, Mew, and Arceus.
- Compatibility and stability fixes accumulated during development testing.
