# Differences from vanilla Pokemon Red

Mars's Expanded Kanto is intentionally an **overhaul**, not a parity patch. The items below are deliberate differences from an unmodded Pokemon Red/Blue game in Gen1Recomp.

## World and progression

* Kanto is expanded with new routes, towns, caves, forests, dungeons, buildings, and postgame locations.
* Existing maps are altered where needed to connect new areas and quests.
* New locations include Route 26, Alabaster Town and Cave, Viridian Deepwood, Lavender Catacombs, Mt. Cinnabar, Celadon Woods, Indigo Highlands and City, Dawn Trail, and Dawn Tower.
* New guards, gates, warps, signs, items, NPCs, and story-state changes alter the original world progression.

## Pokemon

* The Regional Dex is expanded well beyond the original 151 species with selected Pokemon from later generations.
* New species have custom stats, learnsets, evolution methods, encounters, sprites/icons, palettes, cries or cry fallbacks, and Pokedex data as appropriate.
* Several evolution methods are changed to fit Gen 1-style progression and available items.
* Wild encounter tables are rebalanced and expanded across Kanto.
* Selected gifts, trades, fossils, and rare encounters differ from vanilla.

## Moves and battle mechanics

* Many later-generation moves are added or adapted to Gen1Recomp's battle system.
* Original signature moves are added for starters, fossils, legendaries, and other featured Pokemon.
* Numerous signature moves use custom battle animations.
* Some moves intentionally implement mechanics that do not exist in retail Pokemon Red, including custom delayed-hit, defensive, recoil, status, and OHKO behavior.
* Trainer and boss teams are substantially rebalanced.

## Gyms, League, and boss battles

* Gym Leader teams are changed and postgame rematches are added.
* Indigo City has an additional Gym led by Benji.
* A Kanto Fighting Tournament and Master's Tournament are added with temporary level normalization, custom brackets, named opponents, rewards, and final battles.
* Multiple optional superbosses and postgame trainer encounters are added.

## Quests and story

* 21 original side quests are added throughout Kanto.
* The S.S. Anne, Silph Co., Rock Tunnel, Cycling Road, Celadon, Lavender, Alabaster, Pallet, Power Plant, and other areas contain new scripted content.
* New postgame quest lines cover Celebi and temporal anomalies, Ho-Oh and Lugia, Mew/Faraway Island, and Arceus.
* Several vanilla NPC states are extended after major story events.

## Items, shops, and rewards

* New key items, evolution items, quest items, rewards, and move-related items are added.
* Selected shops and prize inventories are expanded.
* Some existing Pokemon use new item-based evolution methods.

## UI and quality of life

* A Journal / Adventure Record screen tracks major events and progression statistics.
* Additional custom screens, quest markers, maps, and presentation effects are used throughout the mod.
* Compatibility shims are included for selected optional sprite/UI mods encountered during development.

## Link compatibility

* The mod changes link-relevant Pokemon and move data and therefore declares `affects\_link: true`.
* Link peers should use the same Expanded Kanto version and compatible mod configuration.

## Scope

* Public v1.0.0 is declared for **Pokemon Red/Blue** only.
* Disabling the mod returns responsibility for vanilla behavior to Gen1Recomp, but saves made with this overhaul may contain mod-specific progression/state and should be backed up before changing mod configurations.
